About SiamBuild Pro

A small team with big standards — bringing European engineering discipline to construction in Phuket.

Our story

Built on trust, delivered with precision

SiamBuild Pro was founded by a certified engineer with over 5 years of experience in construction and project management. After spending years working on large-scale projects, we saw a gap in the Phuket market: clients who wanted quality they could trust, transparent pricing, and someone who actually spoke their language.

We combine deep local knowledge with engineering discipline that most local contractors simply don't offer. Every project is personally overseen by our lead engineer — we don't hand you off to a subcontractor and disappear.

Whether you're building your dream villa or renovating a condo as an investment property, we treat your project with the same seriousness we'd want for our own homes.
