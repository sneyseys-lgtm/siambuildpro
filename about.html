Who we are

Two engineers. One standard.

SiamBuild Pro was founded by two people who've spent years on construction sites that demanded real precision — and got tired of watching that standard ignored on private projects.

Our background

Built on more than just experience

Our lead engineer spent 5+ years working across large-scale projects — residential complexes, industrial facilities, airports, runways, warehouses. The kind of work where cutting corners has real consequences, and where technical decisions have to be justified, documented, and defended.

Our site manager came up through multi-storey residential construction — managing crews, coordinating trades, solving problems on the ground in real time. And both of us have built custom private homes for individual clients in Russia, which taught us something that big projects don't: how to work directly with an owner, communicate clearly, and deliver something that feels personal.

We started SiamBuild Pro because we saw a gap in Phuket. There's no shortage of contractors here. But there's a real shortage of contractors who combine technical rigour with straight communication and actual accountability after the job is done.

That's what we're building — literally and figuratively.

How we operate

What working with us actually looks like

📋

Every project is assessed individually

We don't hand you a price list. We look at your specific project, your site, your goals — and give you a real estimate with a full breakdown. No vague numbers.

🔍

Engineer on every decision

Material choices, structural decisions, MEP design — everything is reviewed by someone with the technical background to know whether it's right. Not just "standard practice".

📱

Weekly updates, always

You get photo and video reports every week. You know exactly what's been done, what's next, and whether anything has come up. No silence, no surprises.

🤝

We're still here after handover

2-year warranty on all work. If something isn't right — we fix it. We're not a company that hands over the keys and stops answering calls.
