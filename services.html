Our services

We don't have fixed price packages. Every project is assessed individually — because every project is different. Tell us what you need, and we'll tell you exactly what it takes.

Service 01

New construction — turnkey

From an empty plot to a finished home. We manage the entire process — architectural design, permits, foundation, structure, MEP systems, interior finishing. One contract, one team, one engineer overseeing every stage.

Our experience spans residential complexes, private homes, industrial facilities and infrastructure projects. We apply that same rigour to every private build we take on — regardless of size.

✓

Design & permits

Architectural and structural drawings, all Thai building permits handled. We know what's required and how to get it done.

✓

Structure & shell

Foundation, frame, roof, exterior walls — engineered for Thailand's climate, seismic conditions, and tropical weather cycles.

✓

MEP systems

Electrical, plumbing, drainage, air conditioning — fully designed, installed, and certified. No cutting corners on systems you can't see.

✓

Interior finishing

Flooring, tiling, joinery, painting, fitted kitchens and bathrooms — to your specification and chosen materials.

✓

Project management

Daily on-site supervision, weekly reports with photos and video, full documentation at handover. You're never left guessing.

💬 Pricing: Every new build is priced individually based on your plot, design, and specification. Contact us to discuss your project — we'll prepare a detailed estimate with full breakdown.

Service 02

Renovation & remodelling

We don't have a minimum project size. Whether you want a full property renovation or just one room redone properly — we'll scope it, price it, and do it right.

A lot of renovation work in Phuket is done quickly and cheaply — and it shows within a year. We do it once, properly, so you don't have to do it again.

✓

Full renovation

Complete gut-and-rebuild of any property — condo, house, villa, or commercial space. New layout, new systems, new everything.

✓

Partial & targeted works

Kitchen, bathroom, flooring, painting, electrical rewire, plumbing replacement — any specific scope. You don't have to renovate everything to work with us.

✓

Commercial spaces

Restaurants, offices, shops, guesthouses, rental properties. We understand the difference between a home renovation and a commercial fit-out.

✓

Investment property prep

Renovating a condo or villa for the rental market? We know what adds value and what doesn't — and we won't oversell you on unnecessary work.

💬 Pricing: Renovation is priced by scope — there's no standard rate because no two projects are the same. Describe what you want done and we'll give you a straight answer.

Service 03

Pools & outdoor living

In Thailand's climate, outdoor space isn't a luxury — it's a significant part of how you live in your home. A well-built pool and terrace adds value, adds enjoyment, and in Phuket's rental market, adds income.

We design and build outdoor spaces that actually work for the tropical environment — proper drainage, the right materials, structures that handle the heat and the monsoon season.

✓

Swimming pools

Infinity pools, lap pools, plunge pools, family pools. Designed to your plot, built to last, with proper filtration and structural engineering.

✓

Terraces & covered areas

Sun decks, sala structures, outdoor kitchens, entertainment areas — designed for how you actually want to use your outdoor space.

✓

Landscaping

Gardens, pathways, boundary walls, drainage — complete outdoor environments that look good and function properly.

💬 Pricing: Pool and outdoor projects are priced based on size, design, and site conditions. Get in touch and we'll assess your plot and give you an honest number.
